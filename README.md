# Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard

Enterprise-Style Power BI Dashboard for Customer, Restaurant, Revenue, and Logistics Analytics

**Tools:** Power BI • Power Query • DAX • Data Modeling  
**Domain:** Food Delivery & Operations Analytics  
**Skills Demonstrated:** KPI Reporting • Customer Analytics • Revenue Analytics • Delivery Analytics • Drill-through Reporting • Interactive Dashboarding • Data Visualization

---

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze end-to-end food delivery operations. The dashboard integrates customer, restaurant, order, and logistics data to provide actionable insights into revenue generation, customer behavior, restaurant performance, and delivery efficiency.

The solution enables stakeholders to monitor operational performance, identify growth opportunities, improve delivery efficiency, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

- Monitor revenue and order performance.
- Analyze customer acquisition and retention.
- Evaluate restaurant performance and customer ratings.
- Track delivery efficiency and late deliveries.
- Identify operational bottlenecks and improvement opportunities.

## Key Insights

- Premium customers represent a small portion of the user base but contribute disproportionately to revenue.
- Approximately 48% of orders are delivered late, indicating significant delivery capacity constraints in high-demand areas.
- Certain cities experience considerably higher cancellation rates, suggesting location-specific operational challenges.

---

## Recommendations

- Prioritize premium customers with faster deliveries, exclusive offers, and loyalty benefits to improve retention and maximize customer lifetime value.
- Improve delivery performance by increasing delivery partner availability in demand hotspots, optimizing routing, and implementing demand-management mechanisms during peak periods.
- Conduct deeper root cause analysis for high-cancellation cities and establish restaurant preparation SLAs to reduce delays and improve service reliability.
